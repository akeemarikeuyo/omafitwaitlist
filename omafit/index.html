Why I'm building omaFIT

Hi, I'm Akeem. I'm a software engineer, so most of my day is spent sitting at a desk. Over time you feel it: your neck pushes forward, your shoulders round, and your back gets stiff long before the weekend.

Most fitness apps assume you have a gym, a free evening and a subscription. I wanted something smaller and more honest: a short posture routine I could actually do every day, strength sessions at home with whatever I had, and a coach that adjusts the plan instead of shouting at me.

So I built it, and I'm its first user. It's free and open source because getting your posture back shouldn't cost money. If you sit for a living too, I'd love for you to try it.

What's inside

01

A posture routine you'll actually do

About 10 minutes a day for your neck, upper back and hips. It doubles as your warm-up on strength days.

02

Strength that builds real muscle

Short sessions around your equipment and level, from bodyweight only to dumbbells and a bench.

03

Walks that fit your week

A gentle walking plan that builds up to easy runs, with a rest timer that tells you when to go again.

04

A coach you're in charge of

Bring your own AI (Gemini has a free tier). It tailors your plan, reviews your week and swaps exercises when something hurts. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.

05

Careful with your body

Log pain by body area and the plan eases off what hurts. Big weight jumps are blocked.

06

Yours, and private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your workouts stay on your phone and only go to the AI you choose.

Already have a gym membership?

Get a personal plan without paying for a personal trainer. Tell omaFIT what your gym has (machines, cables, free weights) and your level, and your AI coach builds your sessions around it. It reviews your progress every week and adjusts your plan as you get stronger, or when something hurts.

Train at home, at the gym, or both. Your plan follows the equipment you pick.

How it works

  1. Set up in a minute. Your level, your equipment and anything that hurts.
  2. Follow today's plan. Posture routine, strength or a walk. Tick sets as you go.
  3. Let your coach adjust. Once a week it looks at how it went and suggests changes.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. omaFIT is free and open source. The AI coach uses your own AI account; Google's Gemini has a free tier, and the workouts and logging work without any AI at all.

What happens to my data?

Your workouts, pain notes and settings stay on your phone. There's no omaFIT account or server. When you use the AI coach, only what it needs goes to the AI provider you picked.

Does it replace a physio or doctor?

No. omaFIT gives training suggestions, not medical advice. It tells you to see a doctor or physio when something sounds serious.

Can I see the code?

Yes. omaFIT is open source, and the code will be public on GitHub when it launches.
