Built for desk days

Hours at a desk show up as a stiff neck, rounded shoulders and a sore lower back. Most fitness apps assume a gym and lots of free time. omaFIT starts from where you are: a chair, a busy week and whatever equipment you have at home, even none.

What you get

  • A daily posture routine

    About 10 minutes for your neck, upper back and hips. It also works as your warm-up.

  • Home strength days

    Short sessions built around your equipment and level, from bodyweight only to dumbbells and a bench.

  • Walks that fit your week

    A gentle walking plan that builds up to easy runs, with a rest timer and alerts during workouts.

  • An AI coach you control

    Bring your own AI (Gemini has a free tier). It tailors your plan, reviews your week and swaps exercises when something hurts. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.

  • Careful by design

    Log pain by body area and the plan eases off what hurts. Every exercise comes from a built-in library with demos, and big weight jumps are blocked.

  • Private and free

    No account and no ads. Your workouts stay on your phone and only go to the AI you choose. Open source.

Already have a gym membership?

Get a personal plan without paying for a personal trainer. Tell omaFIT what your gym has (machines, cables, free weights) and your level, and your AI coach builds your sessions around it. It reviews your progress every week and adjusts your plan as you get stronger, or when something hurts.

Train at home, at the gym, or both. Your plan follows the equipment you pick.

How it works

  1. Set up in a minute. Your level, your equipment and anything that hurts.
  2. Follow today's plan. Posture routine, strength or a walk. Tick sets as you go.
  3. Let your coach adjust. Once a week it reviews how it went and suggests changes.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. omaFIT is free and open source. The AI coach uses your own AI account; Google's Gemini has a free tier, and the workouts and logging work without any AI at all.

What happens to my data?

Your workouts, pain notes and settings stay on your phone. There's no omaFIT account or server. When you use the AI coach, only what it needs goes to the AI provider you picked.

Does it replace a physio or doctor?

No. omaFIT gives training suggestions, not medical advice. It tells you to see a doctor or physio when something sounds serious.

Can I see the code?

Yes. omaFIT is open source, and the code will be public on GitHub when it launches.
